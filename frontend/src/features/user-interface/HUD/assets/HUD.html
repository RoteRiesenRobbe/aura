      How Aura works ✕

      Moving

      Move with WASD or the arrow keys. The minimap is north-up; its compass shows directions. The world is shared — everyone you see is really there.

      Auras

      You fight with auras: a circle around you. No targeting, no aiming — where you stand is the attack. Each aura picks its own targets inside its range, and most affect only a limited number at a time (usually the nearest). An active aura ticks on a steady rhythm — every tick applies its effect; the ring pulse and the pip on the slot show the beat. Enemy auras tick too: stepping out between beats is real play. Exactly one aura is active at a time. You start with Damage equipped.

      Focus

      The bar at the top is Focus — your health and everything you spend, in one value. At zero you die. It refills out of combat; getting hit pauses that for a few seconds.

      Fighting and XP

      Everyone who takes part in a fight gets XP — being close with an aura on counts. The red sword means you are in combat. Mobs stop regenerating for a moment after being hit.

      Levels and skill points

      Leveling up grants skill points. Spend them in the spellbook with +. Higher levels cost more points; each skill has its own cap.

      The three kinds of skills

      Auras: slots 1–3, one active, switch with the hotkeys — also mid-fight. Passives: always on, all in parallel. Cooldowns (Q, R, F): fire once, then recharge — the cooldown belongs to the skill, not the slot. Besides your skills, everyone always has Recall on the utility button: free, no cooldown, but the long cast breaks if you move or get hit.

      Where skills come from

      Granted at certain levels, dropped by some mobs, taught by some people (E when the badge appears), rewarded by quests.

      Equipping

      Click a skill in the spellbook, then a slot or its hotkey. Not while in combat.

      Costs

      Most skills spend Focus — the tooltip states every price. The basic Damage aura is always free: you are never left without an option.

      People and quests

      Get close and press E when the badge lights up. Quests are offered and turned in through dialogue; J opens the journal.

      Dying

      At zero Focus you die and respawn, losing the XP you had gathered toward your next level.

      Dark places and other players

      Some areas are dark — bring a light aura, or a friend who carries one while you carry the damage. Filling roles for each other is the design. There is no PvP.
