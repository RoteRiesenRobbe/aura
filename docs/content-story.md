# Content — Story

Cross-zone story arcs and breadcrumb chains — the *sequence* side of
storytelling. Conventions → `README.md` → Content. World *texture*
(history, factions, tone) lives in `content-lore.md`; a single zone's
internal flow lives in its zone doc (`content-zone-1-farmland.md`,
`content-zone-2-woodland.md`).

## Principles

- **No quest log, no markers** — story is carried by NPC speech lines,
  clue anchor points, and environmental storytelling. A "quest" is an NPC
  telling you something and the world confirming it.
- Breadcrumbs point *outward*: each zone should hand the player at least
  one reason to go somewhere else.

- **Zone 1 → Zone 2 → City:** the family at the homestead points the

  The chain and its quests: `content-zone-1-farmland.md` §2.6,
