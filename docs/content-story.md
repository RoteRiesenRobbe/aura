# Content — Story

Cross-zone story arcs and breadcrumb chains — the *sequence* side of
storytelling. Conventions → `README.md` → Content. World *texture*
(history, factions, tone) lives in `content-lore.md`; a single zone's
internal flow lives in its `content-zone*.md`.

## Principles

- **No quest log, no markers** — story is carried by NPC speech lines,
  clue anchor points, and environmental storytelling. A "quest" is an NPC
  telling you something and the world confirming it.
- Breadcrumbs point *outward*: each zone should hand the player at least
  one reason to go somewhere else.

## Breadcrumb chains (current material)

- **Zone 1 → City:** the Farmer, after the wolf-pack task, sends the player
  toward the City; the tunnel guard warns of the darkness and points to the
  light-aura clue. (Zone-1-internal detail: `content-zone1.md`.)
- **Troll territory:** a clue NPC leads there; the reward is the Heal
  cooldown (→ `content-cooldowns.md`).
- **"Way of the Warrior" sign:** clue location leading to a short dungeon
  with a DPS-aura reward (→ `content-world.md`, unplaced).
