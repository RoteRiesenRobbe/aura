# Content — Cooldowns

Catalog of all cooldown abilities: shipped, designed, and raw ideas in one
table. Conventions (status column, placement split) → `README.md` → Content.
In-game entries: authoritative definition is `api/skills/*.json`; all values

| Dash | in-game | Short forward burst of movement. | Multi-source is safe/idempotent — it has both a teacher and a drop. |
| Swift | in-game *(re-roled 2026-07-29)* | A steerable sprint: the caster moves ×1.5 as fast for ~5 s. First `speed_burst` effect — the movement twin of `tick_rate`. | **Was a `stat_multiplier` passive** until the PO ruling of 2026-07-29 (`plan-playtest-feedback.md` §Open questions 4). | FirstAid *(né Heal, renamed step-7 A.6)* | in-game | Restores the caster's own resource (capped-partial per the §3 recovery boundary). | **The only path to instant self-healing** — heal auras never heal the caster. | Recover | in-game | Heals the caster over ~18 s. | = the "personal recovery cooldown" (GDD §3): the solo sit-and-eat substitute, out-of-combat-flavored. **Self-only** (PO, C8 Session ④). |
| SummonCompanion | in-game | Summons an owned, player-aligned companion beside the caster; follows the owner, fights per the §3.6 assist rules, despawns on TTL. | Mob-depth chunk 6. Taught in-world since C2 Part 2; the companion uses the existing dog SVG. Open questions on XP credit / caps: `backlog.md` §5. |
| SummonTotem | in-game | Summons an owned, player-aligned totem offset from the caster; skill level scales TTL + loadout. | Mob-depth chunk 1. |
| FireTotem | in-game *(2026-07-21)* | Plants a stationary totem that burns **every** enemy around it with a fire dot. | The greater fire elemental's payload. Mechanically the SummonTotem pattern (spawn effect, skill level scales TTL + the totem's loadout level, owner level adds HP/power, cooldown ≥ max TTL = one totem at a time), but the aura hits **all** enemies rather than the nearest: SummonTotem is a single-target poke, FireTotem is **area denial**. `api/skills/fire-totem.json`. |
| Taunt | in-game | Forces every enemy mob in range to the top of its threat table — pulls aggro onto the caster. | Tank role tool (mob-depth chunk 7). Its in-world source beats the group gate that teaches it — beating the drum pays every participant (→ `content-mobs.md`). |
| DamageBurst | in-game *(C4)* | Instant damage to everyone hostile in range, tagged **physical + bleed** — the first multi-tag instant hit. | New skill id 49 (NovaBurst pattern — PO 2026-07-18; NovaBurst itself is a proving-grounds-era design kept alive with a world source, the BanditPyromancer drop). `api/skills/damage-burst.json`. |
| Fade | in-game | Removes the caster's own threat entry on every enemy mob in range — sheds aggro to the next-highest holder. | Group utility, no-op solo (mob-depth chunk 7). |
| Barrier | in-game | Grants an absorb pool to the caster and nearby allies for ~10 s. | Vocab smoke content — **since C7 a combination result** (Hardy + Tough maxed; was cheat-only, closes the §11 "Barrier home" item) → `content-recipes.md`. |
| Shockwave | in-game *(C7)* | §A ceiling trio, **best cooldown**: instant physical+bleed hit at ~2× DamageBurst damage, wider ring, shorter cooldown. | Combination result — `content-recipes.md` (secret in-game). `api/skills/shockwave.json`. |
| CallForAid *(the Boss-Aura, §B)* | in-game *(C6)* | THREE `spawn` effects in one cast — the full squad of 3 SoldierCompanions rises at once (fireCooldown applies every effect; multi-spawn needed zero code). SummonCompanion conventions: skill level scales TTL + summon loadout, owner level adds HP/power; cooldown ≥ max TTL keeps it one-squad-at-a-time by convention. | Below the Vanguard ceiling; C7 combo ingredient (≥3 recipes). The world-boss payload — all participants + recent healers get it (roadmap item 10 machinery). `api/skills/call-for-aid.json`. |
| HoldTheLine | in-game *(C7)* | Tank-support squad: one cast raises 3 ShieldbearerCompanions (RallyDrum allies-only shields) AND detaunts the owner (Fade pattern) — enemies prefer the wall. | FieldMedics | in-game *(C7)* | Support squad: 2 SoldierCompanions + a MedicCompanion (heal aura — heals squad and owner). CallForAid conventions. | Combination result — `content-recipes.md`. `api/skills/field-medics.json`. |
| Haste | in-game | Temporarily increases the caster's aura tick rate. | Vocab smoke content. **The only milestone unlock left** since 2026-07-21. |
| Ignite | in-game | Applies a short fire dot to everyone hostile in range. | Effect-foundations smoke content; the fire line's entry rung and a Wildfire ingredient. |
| NovaBurst | in-game | Instant fire damage to everyone hostile in range, plus a short fire dot. | Vocab smoke content; kept as the wide-but-weak counterpart to DamageBurst. |
| Recall | in-game | Channeled teleport to the last safe place; cast interrupted by damage. | Consumer of `backlog.md` §9 (recall) + the step-3 campfire tracker. |
| Revive | in-game | Channeled: revives the nearest downed player at their corpse with 30% HP; interrupted by damage. | Rare, high-level (GDD §3) — one of the most valuable social abilities. The GDD sketch listed it as an aura; shipped as a channeled cooldown. |
| Calm | in-game *(faction-flips chunk 2)* | Puts **every** eligible mob in the radius out of combat: it drops its target and acquires nothing new — until anything damages it. No faction change; a calmed wolf is still a wolf anyone can hit, it has simply stopped swinging. | **A disengage tool, not crowd control** (D7) — *"stop this fight so I can leave"*, not *"hold this one while I kill that one"*. ⚑ **Any damage breaks it, the calmer's own aura included** (PO ruling): your aura ticks automatically on everything in range, so standing next to a calmed mob with a damage aura up breaks it instantly — switching to a non-damaging aura is the intended play, and the loadout allows exactly one active aura, so that is a real choice. No per-caster exemption; that would be a per-caster relation, which is exactly what the global faction model avoids. **No selector and no `maxTargets` on purpose** — a pack aggros as a pack, so it takes the whole circle. ⚑ Expect a playtester who wants sheep-and-kill to report it as broken; D13's pip is the only feedback that it landed. `api/skills/calm.json`. |
| Paralyze | in-game *(cc-and-retaliation C3, 2026-08-08)* | **The game's first hard stun.** Holds the **nearest** eligible mob for 3 s (→3.8 s at L5): it cannot move, attack, or fire a cooldown. GiantSpider drop at 0.2. | ⚑ **The distinction the whole chunk turns on: a 100 % slow is a ROOT, not a stun.** Movement and aura cadence run on independent paths, so a fully-slowed mob stands still and keeps swinging — the cast half is a separate mechanism (one early return in the SkillSystem's shared cast path). **Damage does NOT break it** (D8): in an aura game your own damage is always on, so break-on-damage would end the stun on the tick it landed — the deliberate opposite of Calm, which any damage breaks. **It keeps the mob's threat table and target** (A5): Calm is the disengage tool, this is the control tool, and a stun that also wiped aggro would be a strictly better Calm. `maxTargets: 1` + `nearest` like CharmBeast — the GDD forbids target-clicking, so walking to the one you want IS the targeting, and an uncapped hard stun would neutralise a pack off one button. ⚑ **No `targetFactions` allowlist**, unlike Calm and CharmBeast: the mob-side `factors.ccImmune` gate (C1) already answers *which mobs*, so every elite and boss is immune and everything else is fair game. ⚑ **Its cooldown timer freezes while YOU are stunned** (A6, if a mob stun ever ships) — a stun costs you time. ⚑ On the wire it lights the **slow** pip, not one of its own: the `applied_effects` ubyte has no free bit and widening it for one buff is backlog §39's job (D6) — so a stunned mob and a slowed one are indistinguishable to the client until then. `api/skills/paralyze.json`. |
| CharmBeast | in-game *(faction-flips chunk 3)* | Turns the nearest eligible mob in the radius into a **full companion** for ~60 s: it follows, defends, assists, and credits its kills to the charmer. On expiry it reverts to its species' exact authored allegiance. | The WoW *Subjugate Demon* model, named by the PO (D6) — not pacification. **It keeps its own level** (D2), which is what makes charming an elite worth a two-minute cooldown; **elites are charmable and faction scope alone decides** (D12 — tier-gating would be the infer-from-a-number pattern entity-model chunk 2 retired). Delivery is `nearest` + `maxTargets: 1` because GDD §9 forbids target-clicking: **positioning is the skill expression**, you walk to the mob you want (D3). **Allied players cannot harm it, and it cannot harm them** (D9) — a straight consequence of the global faction flip, adopted as intent. **You cannot extend a charm** (D11): a second cast passes over it, and during the gap it is hostile again. Breaks on expiry, charmer death **or disconnect** (D10). ⚑ **Known and PO-accepted:** cast into a pack, the pet is immediately focused by its former packmates and can die in ~8 s — that is D9 working as ruled, but it is the first thing a playtester will hit and it will read as a bug. `api/skills/charm-beast.json`. |
| BindElemental | in-game *(faction-flips chunk 3)* | The same charm, scoped to elementals: shorter hold, tighter radius, longer cooldown. | **Its whole job is to prove the mechanism is data.** D8 authors the reachable factions as an allowlist **on the skill** (names resolved to a bitmask at content load — the faction registry is boot-only, so names travel and bits do not), rather than as a per-mob "charmable" flag, precisely so a second charm spell needs **zero Go changes** — it did. ⚑ The PO tuned it differently from CharmBeast **on purpose**, which widens that proof from *"a faction list is data"* to *"the numbers are too"*, and makes a later *charm bandits* a content edit. `api/skills/charm-elemental.json`. |
| Fire Shield | ⚑ **name taken 2026-08-17** | For 30 s, reflects 20% of incoming damage. | Pyromancer combo component. Distinct from FireWard (resist aura). ⚑ **RESOLVED 2026-08-17 by the row below.** The name went to the flat passive (`FireShield`, effect-types C2) and the *timed, proportional* idea this row described shipped as **Retribution** on the new `retaliate_burst` type. Kept for the trail: this row is why both exist. |
| Retribution | in-game *(effect-types follow-up, 2026-08-17)* | For 10 s, reflects 20 % (→40 % at L5) of every hit taken back at whoever landed it, as fire damage. | ⭐ **This IS the row above, built.** The old *Fire Shield* idea asked for a timed proportional reflect and needed code; `retaliate_burst` is that code, and the name moved because `FireShield` had already been taken by the flat passive. Structurally a **`lifesteal_burst` read from the hit side**: activation puts a timed SELF-buff up and the hit site reads it, so it composes with any loadout rather than belonging to one aura. Three PO rulings (2026-08-17): the share is of the **pre-mitigation** swing, so a tanky build does not weaken its own reflect and a fully shielded hit still reflects in full; the damage type is **authored on the skill**, not mirrored from the incoming hit, so a fire-immune attacker shrugs it off and that is the counterplay; and **level buys share, not uptime** (the Bloodthirst rule). It reaches the attacker through the same attributed path FireShield uses, so a reflect-only kill pays XP and kill credit. ⚑ Running it alongside FireShield deals **two separate hits** per incoming swing, each with its own damage type — they are deliberately not summed, because one combined delivery would have to bill one skill's damage under the other's type. ⚑ No pip (the Bloodthirst problem, and worse here: the numbers float off the ATTACKER); the cooldown timer is the tell until backlog §39. Cheat-only for now. `api/skills/retribution.json`. |
| Onward | in-game *(effect-types C4, 2026-08-17)* | A shout: every ally in range moves ×1.4 (→×1.6 at L5) as fast for 5 s (+0.5 s/L). The caster is not buffed - `targetsSelf` absent, the *Fly, You Fools!* stay-behind shape as a burst. | The cooldown twin of the C4 aura, shipped by **extending `speed_burst` with the standard target flags** rather than a new type (plan-effect-types.md D8): same payload, same buff, different delivery - which is why Swift is untouched in behavior (`swift.json` now authors `targetsSelf: true`, pinned identical at the game surface). ⚑ Uncapped on the calm precedent: a shout reaches whoever hears it, and unlike Sanctuary (breadth-per-level because factor 0 cannot deepen) this one levels in strength and duration. ⚑ An ally-only cast reaching nobody is a WHIFF: a mob authoring it keeps its cooldown ready, a player still pays on cast (D9). **No world unlock source yet** by design. `api/skills/onward.json`. |
| Sanctuary | in-game *(effect-types C3, 2026-08-17)* | Makes the nearest ally (one more per level) **immune to all damage** for 5 s. Long cooldown, and it never protects the caster. | The burst half of invulnerability, and the emergency button a healer cannot otherwise press: a heal races the damage, this one stops it. Not a bespoke immunity mechanic - factor 0 has always meant immune, so what shipped is the reserved wildcard tag (`resistTags: ["*"]`, covering every damage tag at once) on a new GENERIC cooldown type, `instant_resist`, the `instant_shield` twin that filled the dispatch grid's empty resist cell (plan-effect-types.md D5). ⚑ The generic type is the point: an ordinary timed fire ward on a cooldown is now content with no code. ⚑ Level buys BREADTH, not depth - factor 0 cannot deepen, so a level protects one more ally. ⚑ It pays on cast, hit or whiff (D9, the cooldown rule), and a running dot on the protected ally is blocked for its whole window too. **No world unlock source yet** by design. `api/skills/sanctuary.json`. |
| Open Portal | in-game *(portal-spells C1, 2026-08-18)* | A 2.5 s wind-up leaves a portal standing beside the caster for 30 s. Any player can press E on it, take the one row, and be delivered to the **caster's** bound campfire. | The first half of the mage-portal / warlock-summon pair, and its niche is moving OTHER players: travelling home alone already ships free as the Recall utility, so this is priced as group transport rather than personal escape (plan-portal-spells.md §1). ⚑ **A wind-up plus a TTL, deliberately NOT a channel** (D1, PO 2026-08-17): cast state rides own-player-only wire fields, so "open while channeling" and "open for 30 s" look identical to everyone except the caster, and no sustain hook exists to build the channel on. The wind-up keeps the interrupt counterplay; the TTL frees the caster to fight. ⭐ Almost none of it is code: the cast is authored (`castTicks` + `castInterruptedByDamage`), the doorway is an ordinary `spawn` with a TTL, and the prompt is the conversation system meeting an inert mob def. The one new verb is the `travel_to` grant, the first conversation grant that hands the player nothing and instead moves them. ⚑ **Level 1 only**, the Recall/Revive/Haste company: a portal either stands where you need it or it does not, and a cooldown that comfortably covers cast + TTL is what keeps a caster to one portal at a time (D7, the FireTotem convention). ⚑ It refuses to cast at all for an unbound caster (`requiresAnchor`, the no-anchor reason at press and at completion): the destination IS the caster's fire. ⚑ The destination resolves when someone steps through, not at cast time (D5), so a caster who re-binds mid-life moves where their own portal leads. **No world unlock source yet** by design (D10). `api/skills/open-portal.json`, `api/mobs/portal-home.json`. |
