    The den

    Spiders' den (new interior file, one mouth)

    Dark all through, webs on the floor (SpiderWeb slows). The one mouth (9) is the way in from the core, with a webbed staging area outside it where a group can see the mouth before going in. The queen sits at the far end; the way out is back through the den and the core (the east mouth was removed, PO 2026-10-25). A group boss: one player carries the torch, the others fight. Base for the queen: BroodMother (fear screech, splits into spiders on death).

    How the light works here

    To scale against a 3 u lane. Lamp (passive, 2.5 u) shows the planks at your feet: walk slowly. Torch (active aura, 4 u) shows the next bend but costs the damage aura. Light is drawing only: a trap hurts whether you see it or not.

    What this draft could not settle

    • Wall geometry. The walls are where the bog ends: deep water as blocking polygons and thicket as blocking paths, kept chunky, never jagged (the collider budget, guide §1). The draft derives them from the paths with noise; the Tiled pass draws them by hand and has to measure the cost.
    • Stacking bog. The bog slows and poisons and stacks up to 3 times (PO). Area effects today refresh rather than stack, so the stack count is engine work.
    • The east exit. The moat runs down the east edge to y 82. The draft puts the exit south of it, a climb down new cliffs into the SE snake water, which is what makes it hard to reach. Southgate's mercenaries (11–12) stand on the other side.
    • The last fire (5). Four fires: entry, the last one before the dark, the gnome's, the pirates'. Today's two (−150, 72) and (−120, 72) both sit where the core goes; the draft moves them. Keep the last fire, or make the core a longer walk without one?
    • Today's air. The NE has a Darkness atmosphere today and the NW a Fog. The draft replaces both: Gloom over the outer labyrinth, full darkness plus a miasma haze over the core, clearings at every fire, the beach in daylight.
