    The two caves

    Kobold cave and bear cave (grown from koboldCave.json)

    Dark (Gloom) all through. Kobolds 7 along both branches. The bear cave has its own mouth (20) and joins the kobold cave inside; one interior file with two surface mouths is the recommendation. The old single exit was the NW mouth; that spot becomes the west branch's way out onto the plateau. The east exit's spot is open; the draft proposes the NW corner tower's guard room.

    Wolf cave (new interior file)

    A small dead-end den behind the SW mouth. A few wolves on the way in, the alpha wolf at the back: one named fight, Fritz's quest. Separate from tunnel.json, which stays the Farmlands' Dire Wolf cave.

    What this draft could not settle

    • The long way to the west gate (Q2). With the bridge broken, the moat cuts the woods off from the city along their whole east side; its only other crossing today is the bridge at its far north end (y −104). Which overland way should a player without the cave walk? Drawn as one candidate: north through the bandit path and Wreckers Bluff to that bridge. A new crossing to the south is the other.
    • Where the cave's east branch comes up. The strip between the moat and the city wall is about 1.5 u wide. The draft puts the exit in the NW corner tower, which is already enterable.
    • The cliff lines. The cliff paths do not say which side is high, so the plateau outline and the bandit path north are read from the line positions. Today's north cliff is unbroken where the old north road ends, so the north brambles need a gap cut into it.
    • Tobias's cart. The burnt cart beside the fire belongs to the Dead Carter (the bandit breadcrumb). The draft gives Tobias a second burnt cart on the road's north bend, where the crate trail starts.
